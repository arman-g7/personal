Blog

I'm a high school student in Isfahan, Iran. For about two and a half years my work has been blockchain engineering — Solidity, smart contract security, Foundry — including competitive auditing on Code4rena and HackenProof, plus freelance full-stack web development (Laravel, React, Node.js) to support myself while I'm still in school.

Right now I'm shifting my long-term focus toward AI/ML. What I actually want to work on is the intersection of AI, healthcare, and neuroscience. For now I'm building the foundations: Python, math (calculus, linear algebra, probability), classical machine learning, then deep learning — mostly through self-study.

I'm already applying it to something real: I'm working on the machine learning and Bayesian optimization side of a friend's pharmacy PhD thesis project, on formulation optimization.

A few other things happened along the way: one peer-reviewed conference paper on post-quantum cryptography migration strategies for Ethereum accounts, and awards at two international invention and innovation exhibitions — the Dubai International Invention Show and the “1 Idea 1 World” competition in Turkey — both associated with Iran's SAMPAD program for exceptional talents.

The plan is to finish a degree at Islamic Azad University while putting most of my real effort into self-directed learning, open-source projects, and research output, and then apply for a fully-funded master's program in Europe.
